# Thumbnail folders

One folder per published paper (folders 01-14). Working papers (conditionally
accepted / in revision / under review) intentionally have no thumbnails —
their visual content/figures may still change before final publication, so
we're holding off until they're formally published.

Each folder now has a PDF of the paper. Claude extracts a candidate figure
from each PDF, crops/resizes it to a thumbnail, and presents picks for
review before wiring up the `image:` field in `_data/publications.yml`.
